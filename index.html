Shikaku: Pocket Rectangles Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026.

Playing and saving

You can play offline. Your progress, selected language, preferences and consent choice are saved on your device. The game works without an account. Sound effects use bundled audio files and do not require microphone access.

Optional game statistics

The welcome screen lets you choose whether to share gameplay statistics. The switch starts on and your choice takes effect after you agree to enter the game. You can change it in Settings. When enabled, the game sends Gemduck a random installation identifier, session and attempt identifiers, language, platform, app version, timestamps, level starts and completions, gameplay activity, moves, mistakes, hints and active play duration. We use these events to understand which puzzles players find difficult and how people use the game.

Network providers handle IP addresses when delivering requests. Our analytics database does not store IP addresses. A coarse country code may be available from the network provider. Gemduck does not match installation identifiers across games or platforms. Events are retained for 90 days and pseudonymous summaries for up to 365 days after activity. Switching statistics off stops new gameplay reporting and clears unsent events. It does not delete data already received.

Game recommendations

The completion screen may request a recommendation and images from Gemduck and look up a suggested game's App Store details through Apple. A tap can open the linked store page. These requests require a network connection. This iOS version does not include a third-party advertising SDK and does not use the advertising identifier or track users across other companies' apps or websites.

Contact and deletion

For privacy questions or to request deletion of previously received statistics, email hello@gemduck.com with the local installation identifier shown in Settings. You can remove local game data by deleting the app. Gemduck gameplay analytics policy.
